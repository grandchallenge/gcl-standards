# Steward-supervised agent staffing

Date: 2026-07-29
Authority: `grandchallenge/INTELLECT/governance/steward_directives/GI-STEWARD-0001.md`
Human Steward: `fyremael`
Status: Effective when the authoritative INTELLECT directive is admitted

## Operating mode

Grand Challenge Labs is temporarily staffed by agents under one Human Steward.
No additional human contributor, owner, reviewer, CODEOWNER, or environment
approver is required during bootstrap.

All non-Steward offices retain their substantive obligations. Reviews must be
exact-revision, evidence-bearing, and tied to an agent identity and invocation
or session. A proposal author cannot supply its own sole verification,
Adversary disposition, or Referee disposition. One agent session cannot occupy
both Adversary and Referee offices for the same decision.

The Human Steward retains every reserved human power and is the sole human
authorization point.

## GitHub controls

The following controls remain active:

- strict exact-head checks;
- resolved review conversations;
- stale-review dismissal;
- protected branches;
- immutable tags and releases;
- no force pushes or deletion; and
- zero bypass actors.

The following are deliberately deferred and nonblocking:

- additional-human approval counts;
- CODEOWNER approval;
- last-push approval by another human;
- human protected-environment reviewers;
- a second human organization owner; and
- human membership thresholds.

Human onboarding and the secure-method-only 2FA account change remain tracked
future work. Paid-plan-only organization rulesets and merge queues remain
optional platform improvements rather than governance activation gates.

## Supersession

This operating record follows `GI-STEWARD-0001`. A later Human Steward
directive must identify which human-independence controls become mandatory,
their transition date, and any grandfathered work.
