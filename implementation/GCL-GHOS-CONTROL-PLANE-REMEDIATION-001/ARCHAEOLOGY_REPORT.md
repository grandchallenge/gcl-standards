## Original thesis

The Constitution, GI-AMEND-0001, ADR-0001, and admitted standards establish a coherent thesis:

1. GitHub is an operational and evidentiary substrate, not constitutional, production-semantic, or mathematical authority.
2. Institutional state must be reconstructible from durable, exact, provenance-bearing records.
3. Issues hold finite operational obligations; PRs integrate reviewed artifacts; checks produce bounded evidence.
4. Authoring, CI, mergeability, and publication do not create authority.
5. Exact-revision review and immutable admission prevent mutable prose or chat from silently changing institutional status.
6. One invocation cannot become proposer, Adversary, and Referee by relabelling itself.
7. Automation may diagnose, validate, route, and transcribe; reserved decisions remain with named authorities.
8. Fresh operators should recover authoritative state without remembered conversation.

The demonstrated failure is narrower than collapse of this thesis: institutional memory was externalized substantially better than execution-control state.

## Known executor limitations and intended mitigations

The protected specification records that bounded horizon, interruption, non-persistent internal state, inability to continue asynchronously, changing tool surfaces, stale context, unguaranteed future discipline, stale evidence, and false role separation were known limitations.

Pre-remediation controls included exact commit/blob/digest/run binding; immutable admissions; stale-review invalidation; distinct Adversary and Referee sessions; Human Steward authority; protected integration/readback; and explicit claim boundaries.

Version `0.2.0` added bounded execution continuity: authorized recovery through non-destructive routes, named stopping boundaries, exact-current identity, superseded-diagnostic rejection, and programme-local recovery procedures. MATH-PROGRAMME's protected recovery guide adds a five-stage diagnostic ladder and narrow repair/replay loop.

Neither layer durably represents a general current phase, open transaction, settled/unsettled gates, deterministic next action, external wait, executor capability class, coordinator state, or crash semantics after every transition.
