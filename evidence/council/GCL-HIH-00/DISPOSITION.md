## Decision interpretation

This is an **optional standards-layer publication convention** recognizing collective human intellectual and creative contributions to AI development and requiring a *separate factual disclosure* when AI materially assists a particular work. It does not identify a model's training corpus, credit any particular author as a proven training example, clear third-party rights, require every GCL programme to conform, or establish a universal legal position.
