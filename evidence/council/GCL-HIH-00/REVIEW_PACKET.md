## Charter and decision

The Human Steward requested that GCL durably preserve an acknowledgment recognizing the historical human intellectual and creative works upon which AI systems build, and put it before Council for potential release. This packet records that mandate; it does **not** claim that release approval has occurred.

**Decision requested:** ACCEPT for protected standards-layer admission and separately authorized release, REVISE with exact required changes, or REJECT with recorded grounds.

**Alternatives considered**
1. Long-form acknowledgment alone: meaningful recognition, but too lengthy for routine work.
2. Short-form acknowledgment alone: feasible for footers, but less explicit about unresolved rights and accountability.
3. Both forms with a separate, factual AI-participation disclosure (proposed): allows adaptable length without replacing provenance or source-level credit.
4. No institution-wide acknowledgment: leaves collective intellectual debt implicit and inconsistent across publications.

**Discriminating tests:** Can authors use both formats without falsely asserting known training-set membership or consent? Can a reader still identify actual AI contributions and accountable authorship? Will applicable third-party rules take precedence? Does the statement remain accurate for opaque models with unknown training data?
