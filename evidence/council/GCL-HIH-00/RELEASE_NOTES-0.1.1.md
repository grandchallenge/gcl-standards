# Human Intellectual Heritage Acknowledgment and AI Participation Disclosure; GCL-HIH-00 v0.1.1

Grand Challenge Labs publishes GCL-HIH-00 v0.1.1, an optional institutional convention acknowledging the cumulative human intellectual and creative work that has helped make contemporary artificial intelligence possible. It provides full and short acknowledgment wording together with a separate template for factual, work-specific disclosure of AI participation and provenance.

Version 0.1.1 is an **editorial successor** to the Council-admitted v0.1.0. It corrects outdated candidate and draft labels. The full and short human-intellectual-heritage acknowledgments, the responsibility clause, and the work-specific disclosure fields remain unchanged. Original v0.1.0 text is retained in immutable historical files.

The acknowledgment recognizes collective human intellectual inheritance. It does **not** establish that any identified author or work was included in a particular model's training corpus, imply consent, compensation, or rights clearance, resolve questions of attribution, or replace direct citations and ordinary scholarly and creative obligations. Accountable authors and publishers remain responsible for their substantive claims, verification, attribution and publication decisions, subject to applicable law and publisher requirements.

GCL-HIH-00 is optional at the standards-registry level. It is not an organization-wide mandatory-use policy; individual GCL programmes adopt it separately if desired. Formal publication of this release does not create new constitutional, mathematical, certification, or programme-adoption authority.
