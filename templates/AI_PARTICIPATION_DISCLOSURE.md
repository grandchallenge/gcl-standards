## Editorial preflight

- [ ] No AI involvement is asserted where none is documented.
- [ ] Material AI contributions are identified to the extent known.
- [ ] All identifiable direct sources have appropriate individual citation or credit.
- [ ] Generated bibliographic references, quotations, calculations, and code have been checked as claimed.
- [ ] Training-data influence is not falsely described as traceable.
- [ ] Author, publisher, and institutional accountability are explicit.
- [ ] Rights, licensing, permissions, and external publication policies take precedence.
- [ ] Heritage acknowledgment and work-specific disclosure remain distinct.
- [ ] Exact work version, review state, and publication authorization are retained.

## Scope note

This is a *disclosure aid*, not a legal opinion, evidence of training-data lineage, a rights clearance, an admission record, an AI-author status policy, or an authorization to publish. An editorial successor becomes admitted through its own protected exact-revision admission record; this template does not independently authorize formal release or require adoption by a programme.
