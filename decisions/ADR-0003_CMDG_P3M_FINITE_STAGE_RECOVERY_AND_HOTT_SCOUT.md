## Decision

Adopt the memorandum as the GCL Codex planning direction, subject to protected admission.

The CMDG successor is governed as:

`CMDG-CM4-P3-M-FINITE-STAGE-RECOVERY-001`

with the sole research purpose of establishing or falsifying whether protected P3-L finite-coordinate dependence forces finite-stage recovery of arbitrary solid-side coefficient morphisms.

P3-M is **not** governed as equivalence transport and must not acquire HoTT, univalence, Cubical Agda, or generic foundation work as a prerequisite unless a concrete local mathematical obstruction is first demonstrated and separately governed.

HoTT remains active only through an independent, non-production scouting control:

`GCL-HOTT-SCOUT-001`.

The scout asks whether a genuinely univalent/cubical formulation materially reduces coherence burden, representation dependence, or proof complexity on a bounded construction already understood by GCL. A successful reimplementation alone is insufficient for promotion.

## P3-M planning boundary

The preferred attack order is:

1. use `kernelProductFunctional_finite_coordinate_dependence` to obtain the finite basis-coordinate set;
2. determine whether that finite set can be captured by one `DiscreteQuotient X`;
3. reconstruct a candidate lower-side finite-stage morphism `gQ`;
4. establish one-point equality with `finiteStageExtension X j gQ`;
5. use protected `coefficient_hom_ext_point` for terminal extensionality;
6. only then conclude `CoefficientFiniteStageMappingOut`;
7. obtain mapping-out injectivity and coefficient-object solidity only through the already-protected equivalence chain.

A negative result is admissible and valuable if it identifies exactly which implication fails or which invariant is missing. New general abstraction requires a demonstrated local obstruction.

## HoTT scout planning boundary

The HoTT scout is firewalled from CMDG P3-M and carries no mathematical or architectural authority over it.

Broader HoTT architectural promotion requires, at minimum:

1. one bounded scout showing a material advantage;
2. a second independent construction showing the same advantage;
3. a clear interoperability boundary with the main GCL formal stack;
4. identified mathematics where higher coherence is a genuine bottleneck;
5. an explicit maintenance/cost model for two foundational ecosystems; and
6. governance rules for transporting claims between them.

Failure of the scout is a valid outcome and should reduce, rather than increase, foundational diversion.
